Security Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

FixaRestudio takes the security of our systems, users, and data seriously. We welcome reports from security researchers and the public.

Scope

This policy applies to systems owned or operated by FixaRestudio, including:

  • fixarestudio.com and its subdomains
  • Applications, APIs, and services we operate
  • Public repositories and infrastructure we manage

Out of Scope

  • Denial of service (DoS/DDoS) attacks
  • Spam, social engineering, or physical attacks
  • Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact
  • Third-party services we do not control
  • Issues requiring unrealistic user interaction or already-known public bugs

How to Report

Email: security@fixarestudio.com

PGP: https://fixarestudio.com/.well-known/pgp.txt

Please include:

  • A clear description of the vulnerability
  • Steps to reproduce
  • Affected URLs, endpoints, or components
  • Proof of concept, screenshots, or logs if available
  • Your name/handle and preferred contact method for credit

Our Commitment

  • We will acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
  • We will provide an initial assessment within 10 business days.
  • We will keep you informed of remediation progress.
  • We will credit you publicly if you want, unless you prefer to remain anonymous.

Safe Harbor

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:

  • Act in good faith
  • Only test within the scope above
  • Avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service disruption
  • Report findings promptly and do not exploit them

Disclosure Timeline

We aim to fix confirmed vulnerabilities as quickly as possible. We ask that you do not publicly disclose the issue until we have had a reasonable opportunity to remediate it. We will coordinate disclosure with you.
