Frequently Asked Questions

What core services does Fixare Studio specialize in?

Fixare Studio specializes in high-performance digital product engineering, custom Web & Mobile app development, AI/ML business integrations, UI/UX product design, FinTech architectures, and scalable cloud infrastructure.

What technology stacks and tools do you build with?

Our tech stack includes modern web frameworks (React, Next.js, Vue, Node.js, Python/Django, Go, Rust), mobile technology (React Native, Flutter, Swift, Kotlin), advanced cloud solutions (AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes), and modern CMS systems (Webflow, Headless CMS).

Can Fixare Studio handle end-to-end product design and engineering?

Yes! We provide full-cycle product delivery—from initial discovery, wireframing, and interactive UI/UX prototyping to full-stack engineering, QA testing, DevOps deployment, and post-launch maintenance.

How quickly can a new project start?

Once scope and agreements are confirmed, our dedicated engineering teams can initiate kickoff within 3 to 5 business days.

Where is Fixare Studio located, and how do you handle remote communication?

Fixare Studio operates as a globally distributed remote studio. We manage real-time team collaboration through Slack, Notion, Figma, GitHub, and Jira, ensuring clear async communication across all global timezones.

Do you work with early-stage startups as well as enterprise clients?

Absolutely. We collaborate with ambitious seed-stage founders building MVPs as well as established enterprises scaling high-throughput applications.

What partnership options are available with Fixare Studio?

We offer strategic co-development, white-label agency execution, technology consulting partnerships, and technical co-founding arrangements for venture-backed startups.

Do you offer white-label design and development for agencies?

Yes. We regularly partner with digital agencies, creative firms, and venture studios as an invisible backend engineering team delivering high-caliber solutions under your brand umbrella.

How does the Fixare referral program work?

We provide competitive referral incentives for partners who connect us with client projects. Reach out via our partner contact form to join our official affiliate network.

How do we get started as an official partner?

Get in touch through our contact section below or reach us directly at hello@fixare.studio to schedule an intro call with our partnership team.

What billing and engagement models does Fixare Studio offer?

We offer flexible pricing options tailored to your project requirements: Fixed-Price milestone contracts for well-defined scopes, Time & Materials for evolving software products, and Dedicated Engineering Teams for long-term support.

Who owns the intellectual property and source code?

You do. Upon project completion and milestone settlement, 100% of all intellectual property, source code, designs, assets, and repositories are fully transferred to your company.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) before project discussions?

Yes, we prioritize confidentiality and intellectual security. We execute mutual NDAs prior to sharing any proprietary documentation or project specs.

How are sprint reviews and progress monitored during development?

We operate in 1 to 2-week Agile sprint cycles. Clients receive weekly status reports, live staging previews, sprint review video demos, and transparent task tracking via GitHub or Jira.

What post-launch support and SLA options do you provide?

We offer comprehensive SLA and maintenance plans that cover bug fixes, infrastructure monitoring, security updates, feature extensions, and 24/7 uptime monitoring.
